    How verified participation improves a locality

    In a full version, a teacher, school club or ward volunteer would confirm that an activity took place. Confirmed activities would raise the locality score, show where help is needed, and let local groups plan the next cleanup.

    In this prototype nothing is verified by anyone. Your counts show your own honour-system actions only.

    Possible future enhancements

    • Teacher or volunteer verification
    • SMS / IVR access for low-connectivity areas
    • Integration with municipal and government portals
    • Real-time civic issue maps
    • Sign-in and school or college groups
    • Backend database and analytics
